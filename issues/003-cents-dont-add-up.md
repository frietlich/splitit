# The "Each" column shows silly numbers and the cents don't add up

## What I did

In **Surf Trip Ericeira** we're three people. Miguel paid €10.00 of tolls on the A8 (the "Portagens A8" expense). I looked at the expenses table to see what each of us owes him.

## What I expected

Something like €3.33. And if you add up what the three of us are down for, it should come to €10.00, not more, not less. If a cent is left over, fine, someone pays it, but the total should be right.

## What actually happened

The Each column says `€3.3333333333333335`. Nobody can pay that. Other rows are odd too: the ice creams (€9.60) show `€3.1999999999999997`, and the petrol (€45.00) shows `€15.0` as if cents were optional. Three times €3.33 is €9.99, so one cent has vanished, and three times the long number is... I don't even know. The amounts themselves are right, it's the per-person share that's messy.
