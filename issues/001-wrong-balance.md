# Balances are off by one share whenever the person who paid is also in the split

## What I did

Opened **Jantar de Curso**. There are three of us in that group (João, Carolina and me) and one expense so far: João paid the €30 dinner at Zé dos Cornos. Then I scrolled down to the **Balances** box.

## What I expected

€30 split three ways is €10 each. João paid, so the group owes him €20 (his €30 minus his own €10), and Carolina and I each owe €10. Something like 20 / -10 / -10.

## What actually happened

The box shows 30 for João and -15 for each of the other two. That would only make sense if João had eaten nothing! It looks like the person who paid is left out of the split, and the others are made to cover his part. I checked in the flat group too: everyone has paid something there, and every single number is bigger than it should be.
